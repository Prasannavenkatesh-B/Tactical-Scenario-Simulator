## 2. Project Monitoring & Review Committee (PMRC)

A high-level Project Monitoring & Review Committee (PMRC) is formally constituted to govern project execution and conduct milestone gate reviews:

1. **Chairman:** Director, Aeronautical Development Establishment (ADE), Bengaluru
2. **Co-Chairman:** Outstanding Scientist / Sc. 'H', CAIR, Bengaluru
3. **Member (Air Tactics):** Group Captain, Tactics and Air Combat Development Establishment (TACDE), Indian Air Force
4. **Member (Naval Systems):** Director, NPOL or designated Senior Naval Liaison Officer
5. **Member (Finance):** Joint Controller of Defence Accounts (JCDA / CDA R&D)
6. **Member-Secretary:** Project Director, Tactical Scenario Simulator (TSS), ADE

### Review Cadence
- **Quarterly Technical Progress Reviews (QTPR):** Verification of intermediate code sprints and integration test benches.
- **Milestone Gate Reviews (M1 to M6):** Strict formal evaluation of contractual deliverables before financial tranche releases. Specifically, Milestone M5 (Month 6) will review the completion of production multi-GPU training and verify that the doctrinal realism score meets or exceeds the $\ge 60.0\%$ contractual requirement.
